# Project: ClearView Optics Singapore

> Example only. ClearView Optics is a fictional brand; names, URLs and rules are illustrative.

## Site
- URL: https://www.clearview-optics.sg
- Markets / languages: Singapore – English (default), Simplified Chinese (promotions only)
- Main audiences:
  - Adults 40+ considering progressive lenses
  - Parents buying lenses for children (myopia management)
  - Office workers concerned about screen time
- Main goal of the site: drive visitors to book an eye check at a partner optical store

## CMS
- Connector: Storyblok
- Space / environment: "ClearView SG – Production"
- Agents may create and edit drafts in:
  - sg/en/promotions/
  - sg/en/articles/
- Agents may only edit (no new pages):
  - sg/en/products/
- Never touch: homepage, navigation, global header/footer, store locator
- Tag every story agents create or edit: ai-draft

## Voice
- Adjectives (3): expert, warm, reassuring
- Spelling (UK / US): UK English ("colour", "optimise")
- Style notes: address the reader as "you"; short sentences; explain any optical term on first use
- Avoid: "eye doctor" (use "eyecare professional"), "cheap" (use "great value"),
  exclamation marks in headlines
- Approved CTAs: "Book an eye check", "Find a store", "Learn more"

## Must-follow
- Claims: only use product performance figures from extras/approved-claims.pdf
- Never say: "cure", "prevent", "guarantee", "best in Singapore"
- Disclaimers:
  - Every promotion: "Terms and conditions apply. While stocks last."
    plus the promo start and end dates
  - Every myopia or eye-health article: "This article is for general information.
    Please consult your eyecare professional for advice."
- Trademarks: ® on first mention of ClearView® and ClearView Kids®
- Competitors: never named

## Approvals
- Approves drafts: Isaac (web), then Marketing (Jamie) for promotions
- Always needs a human before saving a draft:
  - any health or medical statement
  - any new price, discount or percentage
  - any page in Chinese (checked by the SG marketing team)
- Publishing: humans only, in Storyblok
